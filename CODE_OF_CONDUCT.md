# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Macroblock a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give specific technical feedback without attacking people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy, especially because automation scripts may contain sensitive
  text, coordinates, or workflow details.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Trolling, insults, sustained disruption, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Pressuring anyone to share private `.mbk` files or desktop details.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's **Security → Report
a vulnerability** form and begin the report title with `Conduct:`. Reports will
be handled as confidentially as practical. Good-faith reports will not result
in retaliation.

Enforcement decisions will consider the impact, context, pattern of behavior,
and safety of the community. Maintainers will explain decisions to affected
participants when doing so is safe and appropriate.
